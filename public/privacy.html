Privacy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Name a Teammate is a free NBA trivia game. This page says what we collect, why, and who can see it. Questions or deletion requests: nameateammate@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • If you play as a guest: a random guest account and a Guest name, with no email. Guest accounts stay on your device's browser; if you clear it or sign out, the guest account is gone.
  • If you sign up: your email, a username, and a password handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase). We never see your password.
  • Game data: who played, who won, the players named, the era, and turn counts. For accounts we keep win and loss totals.
  • Basic usage data: which page you opened, where you came from (the referring site and any campaign tag in the link), and events such as starting a game or tapping share. These are tied to a random ID stored in your browser, not to your name or email.

Shared game links

When you tap Share, we save that game (usernames, players named, era, turns) so the link can show it. Anyone who has the link can view that recap. Don't share a link if you don't want others to see the usernames in it.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising cookies. We store your sign-in session, your settings, and your random usage ID in your browser's local storage so the game works and remembers you. If we turn on a bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) when you start as a guest, Cloudflare may process your connection to decide whether you are a person.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase for sign-in and the database.
  • Render for hosting the site and game server.
  • Cloudflare for the bot check, if enabled.

We don't sell your data. If we ever add ads or other services that collect data, we'll update this page first.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and game data by emailing us from the address on the account. Guests can clear the site's data in their browser settings.

Children

The game is meant for people 13 and older. If you believe a younger child has created an account, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes

We'll change the date above when this page changes.
